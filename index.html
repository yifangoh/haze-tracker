About the 1-hour figure. NEA publishes the 24-hour PSI and the hourly PM2.5 reading, but not an hourly PSI. The 1-hour PSI here is estimated from the hourly PM2.5 using NEA's PM2.5 breakpoints, so it reflects fine particles only. The 24-hour PSI is NEA's official figure.

Data: NEA real-time PSI and PM2.5 datasets via data.gov.sg, under the Singapore Open Data Licence. Not an official NEA app.
